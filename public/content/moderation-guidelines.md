# Moderation guidelines

These rules are applied to every review and reply before it is published, so that everyone is treated the same way. Moderators decide on the content of a post, never on whether it is positive or negative.

## What is welcome

- Honest opinions about the food, the service and the place, including strong criticism.
- Factual descriptions of what happened, such as a long wait, a cold dish or a wrong order.
- Ratings that match the written review.
- Replies that add something useful to a review.

## What is not allowed

- Personal attacks, harassment or hate speech aimed at staff, owners, other customers or any group.
- Content that is not about the food or the service of the restaurant.
- Personal data, such as phone numbers, home addresses or photos of people who did not agree to be shown.
- Spam, adverts, links to other sites and repeated posts.

## How to decide

- Read the whole post, then check it against the reasons for rejection below.
- If a post is harsh but factual, approve it. Criticism is not a reason to reject.
- When you reject a post, choose the closest reason and add a sentence that tells the author what to change. The author sees your reason.
- If you are unsure, leave the post in the queue and ask another moderator.
